Inbound leads go cold fast. A prospect fills out a form, and if nobody
responds within a few minutes, the odds of a real conversation drop hard.
Most teams still route new leads through manual triage: someone checks a
shared inbox or a CRM view, decides who owns the lead, and pings them
separately. That gap between form submit and a human noticing is where
deals die quietly, with no error and no alert to explain why.

This engine closes that gap. A form submission moves from raw payload to
an owner's Slack notification, with no manual step in between.

## How it works

**Intake.** The form posts straight to an n8n webhook. Every payload gets
an idempotency key at the door, so a double submit or a retried request
never creates a duplicate lead downstream.

**Enrichment.** The lead runs through an enrichment waterfall: try the
primary data provider first, fall back to a secondary source if the first
one returns nothing. A lead never stalls just because one provider is
down or missing a record.

**Scoring.** Enriched data feeds a deterministic scoring step. A lead
that clears the hot threshold routes in seconds, not minutes, so timing
never depends on how busy the scoring logic happens to be that day.

**Routing and alert.** Hot leads route to their owner and land in Slack
with full context attached: who the lead is, what they filled in, and
why the score triggered an alert.

**CRM and nurture.** Every lead lands in HubSpot as a deal, hot or not.
Leads that do not clear the hot threshold enter a nurture queue instead
of getting dropped, so nothing that came through the form goes unworked.
