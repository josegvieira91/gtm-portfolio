A nightly n8n job pulls every stage change and owner touch from HubSpot
into a Postgres mart that keeps the history the CRM forgets. Stage
durations and SLA breaches compute in code, and Metabase reads the mart
directly so every team sees the same numbers.

This one is still in the lab. The pipeline and the dashboards go here once a
night of real stage changes has run through them.
