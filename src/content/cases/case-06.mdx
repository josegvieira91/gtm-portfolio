Every reply in the shared Gmail inbox is classified by OpenAI in seconds:
interested, objection, out of office, or bounce. n8n routes each class,
logs the outcome to HubSpot, and leaves a drafted response waiting in the
rep's inbox for anything worth answering. The full classification prompts
and approval queue ship with the Phase 3 build.
